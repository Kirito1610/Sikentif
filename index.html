    A new hospital, designed for Riyadh

    Sikentif Hospital is a private general hospital in Riyadh, built around three commitments: care that is clinically sound, easy to reach, and clear at every step.

    Safe, clear, connected care

    To provide Riyadh's residents and families with safe, high-quality healthcare that is easy to reach, easy to understand, and continues beyond the visit.

    Riyadh is growing quickly, and its families need private healthcare they can reach easily and trust. Sikentif is a general hospital that combines clinical expertise with a clear, connected patient experience.

    Our vision

    To build a modern healthcare institution in Riyadh that combines high-quality medical care with a convenient, connected and patient-focused healthcare experience.

    What we hold ourselves to

    Safety first

    Every decision starts with patient safety.

    Clarity

    Plain information in Arabic and English, before, during and after care.

    Respect

    Care that respects patients' privacy, culture and preferences, including clinician gender where possible.

    Accountability

    Clear information on costs, waiting times and next steps, and honest answers when something goes wrong.

    Continuity

    Care that connects clinics, diagnostics, pharmacy and follow-up.

    Quality and patient safety

    Quality is built into how the hospital is designed and run.

    • Patient safety first in every decision
    • Evidence-based clinical practice
    • Transparent communication with patients and families
    • Continuous improvement and measurement

    Our services

    Emergency, outpatient, diagnostic, surgical, inpatient and pharmacy services, coordinated around each patient and delivered in Arabic and English.

    Sikentif Hospital opens soon and is not yet accepting patients. For care today, call 997 or 911 in an emergency, or 937 for health advice.

    Emergency care

    A 24-hour emergency department for adults and children, with fast triage, clear communication with families, and direct links to diagnostics and surgery.

    • Triage and treatment around the clock
    • Direct access to imaging and laboratory services
    • Links to specialist and inpatient teams

    Outpatient clinics

    Specialist clinics for consultations, follow-up and ongoing care, bookable online, in the app or by phone.

    • New consultations and follow-up visits
    • Care for long-term conditions
    • Arabic and English at every clinic

    Diagnostics

    Laboratory and imaging services for fast, accurate diagnosis across clinic, inpatient and emergency care.

    • Laboratory testing
    • Imaging including X-ray, ultrasound and CT
    • Results shared securely with your doctor and in the patient app

    Surgery

    Surgical care across a range of specialties, including day surgery where clinically appropriate.

    • Pre-operative assessment
    • Day surgery
    • Post-operative recovery and follow-up

    Inpatient care

    Inpatient wards designed for comfort, privacy and family involvement.

    • Private and shared rooms
    • Family visiting arrangements
    • Discharge planning and follow-up

    Pharmacy

    An in-hospital pharmacy serving inpatients and outpatients.

    • Dispensing for outpatients
    • Clear medicine advice in Arabic and English
    • Prescription refills through the app and website

    Screening & preventive care

    Health checks and screening programmes for individuals and families, focused on early detection and healthy living, with clear package prices in SAR.

    • Health check packages
    • Screening for common conditions
    • Advice on lifestyle and prevention

    We will work with major insurers regulated by the Council of Health Insurance (CHI), alongside self-pay options. Our insurer list will be published here.

      Most companies finish in one sitting if they have the following to hand. Estimates are fine where exact figures aren't available.

      5. Document linksCompany profile, product brochure, pricing sheet and case studies, shared from Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or your website.
